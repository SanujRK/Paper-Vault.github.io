About Nalanda Paper Vault

Centralizing Nalanda past papers for every student.

Nalanda College · Colombo 10, Sri Lanka

Nalanda Paper Vault is an initiative by the 2026/2027 Board of Executives of the Computer Society of Nalanda College (CSNC), created with a clear purpose to make academic resources more accessible, organized, and useful for every Nalanda student.

Why It Matters

For years, past papers have been one of the most powerful tools for exam preparation. Yet, finding them has often been inconvenient, scattered, or limited. Nalanda Paper Vault solves this by bringing everything into one centralized digital platform.

Built by Students, for Students

Nalanda Paper Vault is proudly developed and maintained by the Computer Society of Nalanda College. This project reflects our commitment to using technology to solve real problems within our school community.

Our Mission

To provide a reliable, user-friendly, and comprehensive archive of Nalanda College past papers, empowering students from Grade 6 to Grade 13 to study smarter and perform better.

What We Offer

  • 📚 A complete collection of term test papers across all grades
  • ⚡ Easy browsing and quick access to resources
  • 🎯 Structured organization by grade and exam level (O/L & A/L)
  • 🌐 A platform accessible anytime, anywhere
One Vault. Every Grade. Endless Possibilities.
